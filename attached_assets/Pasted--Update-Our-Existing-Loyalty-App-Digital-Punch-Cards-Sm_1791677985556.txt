# Update Our Existing Loyalty App — Digital Punch Cards & Small Business Promotions

We are building a loyalty and promotions platform for small businesses that do not have their own mobile apps or sophisticated point-of-sale loyalty systems.

**IMPORTANT: Modify our existing application. Do NOT rebuild it from scratch.** First inspect the current project structure, database, user roles, authentication, components, and existing features. Reuse what already works and preserve the current visual design, accounts, data, navigation, and functionality unless a change is necessary.

Our goal is to make the loyalty experience as easy as using a traditional paper punch card, while providing businesses with digital promotions and customer loyalty analytics.

## 1. Main Product Concept

Our application has two types of users:

**Customers**
- Discover participating small businesses.
- Join loyalty programs.
- View digital punch cards for different businesses.
- Request stamps after qualifying purchases.
- Unlock and initiate reward redemptions.
- View available promotions and reward history.

**Business Owners**
- Create and customize loyalty programs.
- Define how many stamps are required for a reward.
- Set up limited-time promotions.
- Track loyalty members and customer activity.
- View stamps issued, repeat visits, and redeemed rewards.
- Manage staff authorization settings.

The platform must minimize the responsibilities of employees during checkout.

We DO NOT want:
- Receipt scanning or receipt uploading.
- QR-code scanning.
- POS integrations.
- Employees searching for customers in a dashboard.
- Employees manually maintaining customers' stamp balances.
- Employees having to keep a merchant dashboard open during checkout.

## 2. Customer-Initiated Digital Punch Cards

Replace the existing merchant-driven stamp-awarding workflow with a customer-initiated workflow.

**New workflow:**

1. The customer opens their digital loyalty card for a participating business.
2. The customer taps a button labeled "Get Stamp."
3. A confirmation screen appears showing the business name and the number of stamps that will be awarded.
4. The employee enters that business's staff authorization PIN to approve the stamp.
5. The backend validates the authorization.
6. If authorized, the customer's stamp count updates immediately.
7. Show a satisfying punch animation and a success message.
8. Automatically record the transaction and update the business owner's analytics.

The employee should not need to search for the customer, open a separate application, or manually change any loyalty balances.

**Important security requirements:**
- A customer must never be able to award themselves stamps.
- Validate the staff PIN securely on the backend, never through hardcoded frontend logic.
- Store PINs securely using a suitable password-hashing method.
- Never expose PINs in API responses, logs, or frontend source code.
- Limit incorrect PIN attempts and temporarily lock out repeated failures.
- Require a new authorization for each stamp-awarding action.
- Prevent duplicate awards caused by double clicks or repeated requests.
- Keep authorization specific to the relevant business.
- Do not allow a customer to enter their own account password as a substitute for staff authorization.

This PIN workflow is a hackathon MVP convenience feature. Structure the code so a stronger staff verification method can be added later.

## 3. Customer-Initiated Reward Redemption

Redemption should use the same simple interaction pattern as awarding stamps.

**New workflow:**

1. When a customer has enough stamps, their reward becomes available.
2. The customer opens their loyalty card.
3. The customer taps "Redeem Reward."
4. Show the reward details and ask the customer to confirm that they want to redeem it.
5. The employee enters the business's staff authorization PIN.
6. The backend validates the PIN and confirms that the reward is eligible.
7. The system atomically deducts the required stamps and records the redeemed reward.
8. Display a clear, visually appealing redemption success screen showing the reward and business name.
9. Update the customer's wallet and the merchant's analytics immediately.

Redemptions must not be completed solely by a customer clicking a button.

Ensure:
- Rewards cannot be redeemed without sufficient stamps.
- One redemption cannot be processed twice.
- Failed authorization does not remove stamps.
- Redeemed rewards appear in transaction history.
- Customers see their updated stamp balance after redemption.
- The merchant dashboard accurately reflects redeemed rewards.

## 5. Business Loyalty Program Management

Business owners should be able to create and edit their own digital punch-card programs.

Allow owners to configure:
- Business name, logo, and description.
- Loyalty program name.
- Required stamp count, such as 5, 8, or 10.
- Reward description, such as "Free Coffee," "Free Haircut," or "$5 Off."
- Basic program terms.
- Whether the loyalty program is active or paused.

Changes should save to the existing database.

Do not erase customers' existing stamp balances when a business edits its program. Preserve existing progress and handle changes to reward thresholds consistently.

## 6. Promotions — Important Standout Feature

Add a promotions section to the business dashboard.

Business owners should be able to create, activate, schedule, and deactivate promotions without requiring employees to perform extra steps.

For the MVP, include these promotion types:

**A. Double Stamp Promotion**
Example: "Double Stamp Tuesday"

During the configured promotion period, one authorized punch automatically awards two stamps instead of one.

Employees still complete only one approval.

**B. Limited-Time Offer**
Example: "Weekend Special — Earn 2 Stamps on Every Visit."

The offer appears on the customer's loyalty card and in the promotions section.

**C. Bonus Stamp Promotion**
Example: "Grand Opening — Earn 1 Extra Stamp With Your Next Purchase."

The system automatically applies the eligible bonus when a stamp is authorized.

Promotion settings should include:
- Promotion title.
- Description.
- Start date and end date.
- Applicable business.
- Stamp multiplier or bonus.
- Active/inactive status.

**Business rules:**
- Check promotion eligibility on the backend at the time of the transaction.
- Show the expected number of stamps before approval.
- Apply only eligible promotions.
- Prevent unintended stacking of promotions.
- Record which promotion was applied.
- Prevent a promotion from awarding stamps more than once for the same authorization request.

The promotion should never require an employee to manually calculate bonus stamps.
