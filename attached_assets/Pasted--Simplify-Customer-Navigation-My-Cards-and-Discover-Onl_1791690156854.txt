# Simplify Customer Navigation — My Cards and Discover Only

Modify our existing LoyaltyHub application. We want to simplify the CUSTOMER experience by eliminating the separate Home page.

IMPORTANT: Do not rebuild the application. Preserve the current design, database, customer accounts, stamp balances, loyalty functionality, and merchant-facing screens.

## 1. Customer Navigation

The customer side should have ONLY TWO bottom navigation tabs:

- My Cards
- Discover

Remove the Home tab from customer navigation.

Make My Cards the default landing screen when a customer signs in.

Update navigation and routing so customers no longer depend on the old Home screen.

Do not change merchant navigation or merchant dashboard functionality.

## 2. My Cards Page

Make My Cards the customer's main dashboard.

At the top, display:

"Hello, [Customer's First Name]!"

Subtitle:
"Your local rewards, all in one place."

Remove the large "Your Perk Points" section and its associated display from the customer experience.

Below the greeting, display all loyalty cards belonging to the customer.

Each card should show:
- Business name and logo.
- Loyalty program name.
- Current stamp balance.
- Number of stamps needed to unlock a reward.
- Filled and unfilled stamp indicators.
- Reward description.
- Number of stamps remaining.
- Available reward indicator when eligible.
- Existing Get Stamp button.
- Existing Redeem Reward button when eligible.

Display cards with rewards ready for redemption first.

Do not change existing stamp-awarding or redemption authorization logic.

## 3. No Loyalty Cards Empty State

If the customer hasn't joined any businesses, display:

"Your rewards start here!"

"You haven't joined any loyalty programs yet. Discover local businesses and start earning rewards."

Add a button labeled "Discover Businesses" that navigates directly to the existing Discover tab.

Do not display blank cards or fabricate loyalty programs.

## 4. Collapsible Past Activity

On the My Cards page, add a section titled "Past Activity" underneath the loyalty cards.

Make it a collapsible dropdown that is closed by default.

When expanded, show:
- Business name.
- Date of transaction.
- Stamps earned.
- Bonus stamps earned, where applicable.
- Rewards redeemed.

Use actual existing transaction history.

Sort newest first.

If there is no activity, display a friendly empty-state message.

## 5. Discover Page

Keep the existing Discover page design.

Make sure merchants the customer has already joined are not shown in recommendation sections such as:
- "A Place to Try Next"
- "Find Your Next Fav"

Combine duplicate recommendation sections if appropriate.

Prioritize businesses the customer has NOT joined.

Already-joined businesses may still appear in general search results, but they should have a "Joined" indicator rather than being recommended as new businesses.

Prevent duplicate merchant recommendations.

If there are no new participating businesses to recommend, display:

"You're all caught up!"

"You've discovered all participating businesses for now. Check back for new local spots!"

Do not display an empty section or create fake merchants.

Customers should still be able to browse active promotions and join available loyalty programs.

## 6. Design Requirements

Keep the current LoyaltyHub visual identity:
- Cream background.
- Dark green primary color.
- Soft green accents.
- Rounded cards.
- Existing typography.
- Mobile-friendly design.

The bottom customer navigation should show exactly two tabs: My Cards and Discover.

Do not add a new homepage.

## 7. Preserve Existing Functionality

Do not change:
- Merchant dashboard.
- Merchant loyalty management.
- Staff PIN authorization.
- Stamp-awarding logic.
- Reward redemption logic.
- Business promotions.
- Existing customer accounts and balances.
- Backend authentication.

Do not add receipt scanning or QR-code scanning.

## 8. Testing

Verify that:
1. Customers land on My Cards after signing in.
2. Only My Cards and Discover appear in customer navigation.
3. Existing loyalty cards and stamps remain accurate.
4. Get Stamp and Redeem Reward still work.
5. Customers without cards can navigate to Discover.
6. Already-joined businesses are excluded from new-business recommendations.
7. The Discover page continues to function.
8. Past Activity expands and collapses.
9. Merchants can still access all existing merchant pages.
10. Everything displays properly on mobile.

Only implement these customer navigation and page organization changes.

Do not modify the merchant retention dashboard yet.

Before making changes, inspect the existing navigation and screen components to determine what can be reused. After implementation, summarize the files changed and tests completed.
