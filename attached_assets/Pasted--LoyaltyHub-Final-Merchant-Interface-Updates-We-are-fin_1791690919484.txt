# LoyaltyHub — Final Merchant Interface Updates

We are finalizing our existing LoyaltyHub mobile app for a hackathon.

**IMPORTANT: Modify ONLY the merchant interface. The customer interface is already complete and must remain unchanged.**

Do not rebuild the application, redesign the interface, reset the database, or remove working functionality.

Preserve the existing cream background, dark green colors, lime accents, typography, rounded cards, and mobile-first design.

The merchant interface must have exactly THREE navigation tabs:
1. Overview
2. Manage
3. Members

Our goal is to provide small-business owners with a simple way to manage loyalty programs, create promotions, and understand customer retention without adding unnecessary work for employees.

---

## 1. MERCHANT OVERVIEW PAGE

Keep the existing Overview page design and layout wherever possible.

### Remove unnecessary elements

Remove the following from the Overview page:

- Approved Visits
- Rewards Available Now
- Rewards Earned
- New Members
- Visits This Week graph
- A Reason to Come Back
- View Members shortcut
- Recent Loyalty Members section

Do not delete underlying database records or functionality when removing these interface elements.

Keep the other existing useful merchant metrics and controls, including loyalty members, stamps rewarded, rewards redeemed, repeat customers, and active promotions.

### Interactive Repeat Customers Analytics

Under "Your Loyalty at a Glance," make the existing **Repeat Customers** metric clickable.

When clicked, open a mobile-friendly analytics modal or expanded detail view that shows customer activity and retention over the past 30 days.

Include:

**Repeat Visit Rate**

Calculate the percentage of unique customers with at least one approved loyalty visit in the last 30 days who made at least two distinct approved visits during that period.

Formula:

(Unique customers with 2+ approved visits in the past 30 days / Unique customers with 1+ approved visits in the past 30 days) × 100

**Customer Activity Trend**

Display a line chart showing the number of unique customers with approved loyalty activity, grouped by week over the past 30 days.

**Repeat Visit Trend**

Display a weekly trend of the percentage of active customers who made at least two separate approved visits within the corresponding week.

**Period Comparison**

Compare the latest 30-day repeat visit rate and number of active customers with the previous 30-day period.

Show:
- Increase, decrease, or no change.
- Percentage-point change for repeat visit rate.
- Numerical change for active customers.

When historical data is insufficient, display "Not enough historical data" instead of inventing results.

### Important Calculation Rules

- Use real existing transaction records.
- Count one authorized punch transaction as one approved visit.
- Double-stamp promotions must still count as only ONE visit.
- Reward redemption does not create an additional visit.
- Use only data belonging to the selected merchant.
- Do not hardcode chart data.
- Show appropriate empty states when there is no activity.
- Keep the analytics readable on mobile.

### Move Recent Reward Activity

Remove "Recent Reward Activity" from the Overview page.

Move it to the Members page, preserving its data and functionality.

Do not duplicate it across pages.

## 3. MERCHANT MEMBERS PAGE

Keep the current Members page styling and general layout.

### Add Member Sorting

Add a "Sort By" dropdown beside or directly below the existing member search bar.

Provide the following options:

1. Most Recently Joined — default.
2. Oldest Members.
3. Most Recent Visit.
4. Most Visits.
5. Name A–Z.

Use actual membership creation timestamps and approved loyalty-visit records.

Customers without recorded visits should appear last when sorting by Most Recent Visit.

Ensure sorting works alongside the existing search functionality.

Sorting must apply only to the currently selected merchant.

### Remove Points From Member Activity

Remove "Points" and lifetime perk point totals from individual member cards.

Instead, display:
- Customer name.
- Total approved loyalty visits.
- Current stamp balance.
- Progress toward the next reward.
- Last approved visit date, when available.
- Rewards redeemed, where relevant.

Use actual existing data.

Do not delete customer stamp balances or loyalty histories.

### Recent Reward Activity

Move the existing "Recent Reward Activity" section from the merchant Overview page to the Members page.

Place it below the member list.

Show recent:
- Approved stamp awards.
- Bonus stamp awards.
- Reward redemptions.

Each record should display:
- Customer name.
- Activity type.
- Transaction date.
- Stamp change, if applicable.
- Reward name when applicable.

Sort newest first.

Use the existing transaction history and do not fabricate records.

Show a friendly empty state if there is no activity.
